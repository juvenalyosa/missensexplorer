# ACMG PP3/BP4 calibration of the MissensExplorer v2.5 score

Generated by `benchmarks/calibrate_acmg.py` on 2026-11-26: the method of Pejaver et al. (AJHG 2022) that set ClinGen's thresholds for REVEL and other tools, 10,000 bootstrap samples.

- **Calibration data**: the paper's ClinVar 2019 set, 11,201 variants (5,361 pathogenic, 5,840 benign, 1,825 genes), each scored by a model fitted without its gene.
- **gnomAD set**: 5,615 rare gnomAD v4 missense variants not in ClinVar, from 489 genes.
